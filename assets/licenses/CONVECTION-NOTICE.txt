Optional Convection / Xbox TC glyph resources

The separate artwork pack may contain a raster coverage atlas and advance
metrics recovered from the user's Convection.Font.for.Xbox.360.JTAG+RGH.zip.
The original main resource identifies its family as "Xbox TC". The archive
author describes these as the original Convection console fonts. Font rights
remain with Microsoft and the respective rights holders. These glyphs are
not covered by the project's MIT licence.

Input: xenonclatin.xtt (1,159,168 bytes)
SHA-256: 977bbe13d5545d300e57dacf9dff492476452f50880ef3868d3aa308d041187d
All 271 compressed glyph-page SHA-1 values match the input xchk table.

The source is paged zlib-compressed font data, not an encrypted font.
An external, GPL-3.0 conversion tool was used offline:
https://github.com/taglocklar/360dashboards/blob/ad85c697bde95022cf3c9425bb0b51e7c4a9f1e9/tools/xtt2ttf.py
That converter is not incorporated into or linked with the executable.
The pack includes coverage for the application's 95 printable ASCII
characters, with fractional advances. It contains no raw TTF/XTT files.

The executable retains its Apache-licensed Open Sans fallback. No console
system-font installation or flash modification is required.
