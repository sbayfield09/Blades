Transition recordings in the separate artwork/audio pack come from
Assets/Audio/Sounds in the same pinned repository revision. Used files:
blades-switch-marketplace-to-live.wav, blades-switch-live-to-games.wav,
blades-switch-games-to-media.wav, blades-switch-media-to-system.wav,
blades-menu-open.wav and dash_2ndLevelClose.wav. Xbox/Microsoft and the
respective rights holders retain ownership of these sounds. Recordings are
converted to PCM16 stereo at 48 kHz; the XEX embeds none of these recordings.

The v0.16 Xbox Live panel texture adapts the rounded grey Border in
XboxMetroLauncher/MainWindow.xaml and the ring colour/stroke from
XboxMetroLauncher/XboxMetroLauncher.Controls/BladeRingBackground.cs, inspected
at repository revision 7d92705cf2131e6159120cfa5ae10736ddfabf68. Geometry,
neutral colour, bevel, ring spacing and outer shadow follow the user-supplied
reference. This is adapted vector artwork, not an extracted retail bitmap.

Version 0.17 imports Assets/Blades/Startup/anim-left-runner.png and
anim-right-runner.png, cropped after conversion to the 1280x720 design grid.
The initial radial reveal and side-leaf staging adapt MainWindow.xaml,
MainWindow.cs and BladesArtworkHost.cs in the same project at pinned revision
0c8adb2cef4ad7dff2bf889351efa1f1c244aef2. Shutter positions are measured from
the user-supplied reference recording. Startup reuses dash_2ndLevelClose.wav
without pitch modification; all previously supplied recordings are unchanged.

Version 0.18 adapts the console silhouette, sixteen-dot orbit and 1.7 Hz
timing from XboxMetroLauncher/XboxMetroLauncher.Controls/BladesLoadingIndicator.cs
at pinned revision 0c8adb2cef4ad7dff2bf889351efa1f1c244aef2. The small transparent
frames use the dark indicator treatment observed in the user's disc-reading
video. They are prepared in the premade pack and played through the existing
GPU compositor; no video footage is embedded.
